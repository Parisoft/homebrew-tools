USAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    export CC65_HOME=<this folder>
    export PATH="$CC65_HOME/bin:$PATH"

CC65_HOME is REQUIRED: it must point at the directory that contains include/,
lib/ and cfg/. Both <this folder> and <this folder>/share/cc65 work because of
the symlinks above. PATH alone is not enough - cc65 2.18 has no fallback to a
path relative to its own binary, so without CC65_HOME it cannot find its headers
or libraries.
